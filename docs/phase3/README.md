# Phase 3 — human-validation packet and time-saved methodology (G13–G14)

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `review-packet/` | the G13 deliverable: a self-contained packet a senior platform engineer can review in 30–45 minutes — 4 rendered reports over the recorded offline fixtures, environment inputs, questionnaire, machine-ingestible answer sheet, pre-registered scoring, citation-verification record, reproduce script, and two clearly-labelled AI proxy reviews |

| `pilot/` | the two upgrade plans produced by the pilot's two arms (`traditional-plan.md`, `ri-plan.md`), kept side by side |

Everything here was generated from state that already exists on this branch
(the recorded offline fixtures under `internal/app/testdata/e2e/state` and
`internal/app/testdata/impact-llm-cache`, plus copies of the environment
fixtures under `review-packet/example-env/`); no files outside `docs/phase3/`
were created or modified.

Honesty labels used throughout: **proxy ≠ human** (the two reviews are
AI-simulated), **pilot ≠ study** (n=1, AI operator, contaminated), and every
number carries its n.
