Reading:

- **R12 is a real recall gap of the diff model.** A Role or ClusterRole that is new
  under its name is one `resource-added` record, so its permissions do not appear
  as `rbac-permission-added` changes. The rendered object holds them, and the
  `rendered-diff` validator's inventory reads them. A future option is to also
  emit per-permission records for added/removed roles; the trade-off is noisier
  output.
- **CRDs:** the earlier "R16 known gap" was a misread of the chart. cert-manager
  ships its CRDs as a gated template (`crds.enabled`, default false), so they
  appear only in the variant, where the new `signatureAlgorithm` field is
  reported. The rotationPolicy default change has no schema default (R17
  holds); it is not render-verifiable.
- **Precision 14/14** at release level: every rendered change is explained by an
  upstream-authored expectation. The renderer's own changelog correlation marks
  6 of the 14 as documented by an edge change (images and the targetPort default).
  The other 8 (RBAC split, probe ports) are undocumented, so they go to review.
